Free tools to protect yourself
  • Check a broker or advisor: FINRA BrokerCheck (brokercheck.finra.org) and the SEC's adviser search (adviserinfo.sec.gov)
  • Learn the basics and spot fraud: Investor.gov, from the SEC
  • Check if a bank is FDIC-insured: FDIC BankFind (fdic.gov)
  • Report investment fraud: sec.gov/tcr, ReportFraud.ftc.gov, or your state securities regulator (find yours at nasaa.org)

About

Red Flag or Green Light: The Investing Reality Game is a free investing-awareness game from WGRALGO / The Wealth Gap Resolution Algorithm™ Inc. Learn to tell real opportunities from polished traps.

How it works

  • Pick a level: All Levels, Beginner, Everyday, or Expert.
  • Each round has 10 questions. 🚩 / 🟢 questions ask if a real-world situation is a trap or a sound money move. 🧠 questions test how investing really works.
  • Every answer explains why, and the results show how you did by topic.

For education only, not financial advice. No investment is guaranteed, and past performance does not predict future results.
